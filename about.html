About

Minimal Time + Count is a small browser utility for counting time, things, or both.

Three simple modes

  • Focus — configurable focus sessions, short breaks, and long breaks.
  • Counter — a straightforward manual tally.
  • Timed Counter — count events while a countdown is running.

Design principles

The app is intentionally minimal: no account, no subscription, no premium tier, and no unnecessary feature clutter. Settings are stored in your own browser so the tool can remember your preferences without requiring a server-side profile.

The core utility is intended to remain free to use.

Private by design · No account · Your data stays local · Free forever
