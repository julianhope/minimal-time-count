Privacy

Last updated: 2026-09-13

No account

Minimal Time + Count does not require you to create an account or provide a name, email address, or other profile information.

Local browser storage

The app uses your browser's local storage to remember preferences such as timer durations, break settings, auto-start choices, and sound settings. This information stays in your browser and is not sent to an application database.

Active timers and live counter values are not designed to be restored after you close the browser.

No app-side analytics

The app itself does not currently include analytics or behavioural tracking code.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure. As with ordinary web hosting, Cloudflare may process technical request information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP addresses, request metadata, or security logs, according to Cloudflare's own policies.

Advertising

This version of the site does not currently display third-party advertising. If advertising is introduced, this privacy notice will be updated before it is enabled, and any legally required consent controls will apply to the advertising layer rather than restricting access to the utility itself.

Clearing saved settings

You can remove locally stored settings by clearing site data for this website in your browser. Doing so resets saved preferences to their defaults.

Changes

If the site's data practices change, this page will be updated to describe the new behaviour.
